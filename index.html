THE CODE OF CONDUCT

Server Rules

  • Respect all players and staff members at all times.
  • No harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior.
  • English and Arabic are the primary languages in-character.
  • Do not impersonate staff or other community members.
  • Follow the chain of command when reporting issues.
  • Stay in character at all times while in the server.
  • No Random Deathmatch (RDM) — you must have valid RP reason.
  • No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM) — vehicles are not weapons.
  • No metagaming — do not use out-of-character information in RP.
  • No powergaming — do not force actions on other players.
  • All criminal RP must be well-organized and realistic.
  • Robberies require a minimum number of online police officers.
  • Gang wars must be approved and declared through RP channels.
  • No cop-baiting or provoking police without valid RP reasons.
  • Hostage situations have a maximum duration of 30 minutes.
  • After respawning, you forget all events leading to your death.
  • You may not return to the area of your death for 15 minutes.
  • You cannot seek revenge on the person who killed you.
  • Medical RP can override NLR if properly roleplayed.
  • Use proximity voice chat for in-character interactions.
  • Radio frequency use must be realistic and limited.
  • No soundboards, voice changers for trolling, or mic spam.
  • Discord calls during RP are considered metagaming.
  • Zero tolerance for hacking, modding, or exploiting.
  • Report all bugs and glitches immediately to staff.
  • Using any third-party tools for unfair advantage is a permanent ban.
  • Duplication of items or money will result in a wipe and ban.
